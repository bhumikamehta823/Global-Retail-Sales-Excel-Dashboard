# Global Retail Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard analyzing global retail performance, customer demographics, shipping trends, and product category sales.



## Project Overview
This project transforms raw retail transaction data into an interactive visual report using Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers. It allows stakeholders to filter sales metrics dynamically by shipping status, year, and day of the week.

## Key Metrics Tracked (KPIs)
* **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated ($48,382.00 under current slicer selection).
* **Total Quantity Sold:** Total units purchased (474 units under current selection).
* **Average Selling Price:** Mean unit price across orders ($101.70).
* **Average Shipping Cost:** Mean delivery expense per order ($47.30).
* **Average Customer Rating:** Overall satisfaction score (3.6 / 5.0).

## Dashboard Visuals & Insights
* **Top Sales Locations:** Compares revenue share across major cities including Sydney (23.48%), Dublin (23.45%), Mumbai (19.50%), London (17.53%), and Liverpool (16.05%).
* **Product Category by Quantity:** Shows volume distribution across Clothing (48%), Ornaments (36%), and Other items (16%).
* **Customer Rating Distribution:** Breaks down customer feedback from 1 to 5 stars, with 4-star (30.04%) and 5-star (31.14%) reviews making up over 61% of ratings.
* **Monthly Trends of Sales:** Tracks revenue fluctuations from January through October, highlighting a peak in May.
* **Sales by Product Category and Gender:** Compares purchasing volume between male and female shoppers across categories.
* **Quantity Sold by Age Group:** Segments unit volume across age brackets (18–20, 21–25, 26–30, and 31–35), with the 31–35 group leading demand.

## Interactive Features
* **Slicers:** Filter the entire dashboard dynamically by **International Shipping Status**, **Year**, and **Day of the Week**.
* **Workbook Tabs:** Organized cleanly into three sheets (`data`, `dashboard`, and `pivot charts`).

## Tools Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and interactive Slicers.
